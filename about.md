Chess Arena is a lightweight browser chess game designed for quick local matches without accounts or setup.

## Game modes

- **Local PvP** lets two players share the same device.
- **PvE** lets you play as White or Black against a beginner-friendly computer opponent.

## How to play

Select a piece to view its available moves, then select a highlighted square to move. You can restart a game at any time, or undo the latest full turn after at least two moves have been played.

The game runs entirely in your browser and can be installed as a PWA for a more app-like experience.
